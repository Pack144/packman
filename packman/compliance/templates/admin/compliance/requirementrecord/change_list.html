{% blocktranslate %} A record is one Cub, adult, or family's standing against one requirement in one pack year. Records are normally opened in bulk from the Requirements page at the start of the year, then filled in here as paperwork arrives. This list opens on the current pack year; use the "pack year" filter to look back at a previous one. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Recording something" %} {% blocktranslate %} — set the status to Complete and fill in the date it was completed. If the requirement expires and has a "valid for" duration configured, the expiration date is suggested for you; otherwise enter the date printed on the card or form. Use Waived when a member does not need to satisfy the requirement at all, so it stops being counted as outstanding without pretending it was collected. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Expiring and Expired are not statuses you set" %} {% blocktranslate %} — they are worked out from the expiration date every time a page is loaded. A completed record becomes expiring, then expired, on its own as the date passes. Nothing needs to be re-saved, and there is no end-of-year cleanup to remember. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Someone who joined mid-year" %} {% blocktranslate %} — a member who arrives after the records were opened simply has no row here yet. Re-run "Open records for the current Pack Year" from the Requirements page to pick them up; it skips everyone who already has one. A member's records can also be edited directly on their own page under Membership. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate %} Notes are for tracking the paperwork itself — who it was handed to, which form is still missing. Never record medical details or anything else from the contents of a form here; the pack only needs to know whether it has been collected, not what it says. {% endblocktranslate %}
